How to play

  • 🏠 Four customers sit at the belt. Each wants a set amount of one dish from the menu.
  • 🍳 You're the server. Pick dishes from the 4 kitchen lines — tap a line (or press 1–4) and its front item pops onto the belt.
  • 🔁 The belt loops forever. A dish is served when it passes a customer who ordered it — anything else just keeps rolling around.
  • ⏳ Customers have patience bars. If one empties, they storm out. 3 angry customers = game over.
  • 🚧 The belt has a max capacity. If it's full and none of the dishes match what anyone wants — belt jam, game over. Only dish what gets eaten!
  • 🎯 Reach the serving target to clear a level. 50 levels — guests can demand up to 10 of a dish at the top, and the belt limit grows to match. A level only counts as done once the very last plate has reached its guest — no closing up while food's still on the line.
  • 🏷️ Every level is a fixed kitchen: the same orders arrive every time, and the kitchen only makes exactly what its roster orders. Finish clean and the last dish is eaten as you win — no endless supply.
